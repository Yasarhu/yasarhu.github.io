01 — PROFILE

About

Nine years across advertising and EdTech, now leading creative at Crio.Do.

A Creative Head operating across five connected disciplines — video, design, brand, content and AI — rather than any one of them in isolation.

Currently leads the complete creative division at Crio.Do: directing video production and post-production, setting visual design and UI/UX standards, governing brand identity across every touchpoint, shaping content strategy for course and marketing output, and running the generative-AI pipeline that ties all four together. That range is what the Creative Head role actually demands — a campaign has to look right, sound right, read right, move right, and now increasingly, scale through AI without losing any of the first four. Background spans agency brand and commercial work at 7MPS and senior video leadership at Skill-Lync, building equal fluency in design systems, production craft and brand discipline before AI entered the workflow.
